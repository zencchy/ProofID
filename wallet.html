Security Centre

Information about how this ProofID prototype protects your credentials.

Issuer signature verification

Credentials are digitally signed by the issuer. The backend checks the signature to detect unauthorised changes.

Private key protection

The issuer's private signing key is stored on the backend in this prototype. It should be secured separately in production.

Wallet encryption
